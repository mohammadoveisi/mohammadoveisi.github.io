Research

I design RF, analog, and mixed-signal integrated circuits and carry them from system-level modeling through transistor-level design, layout, tape-out, and silicon measurement. My Ph.D. research in the NCIC Labs at UC Irvine, advised by Prof. Payam Heydari, covers the projects below.

Antenna-to-Bits F-Band 120-Gbps 64-QAM CMOS Receiver

22-nm FD-SOI · JSSC 2026, VLSI 2025

A fully integrated 100–140-GHz receiver that takes the signal from the antenna to the output bitstream and supports QPSK, 16-QAM, and 64-QAM. I developed the analog-domain 64-QAM detection and bit-extraction circuits, which remove the need for power-hungry high-speed ADCs and DSP. The receiver demonstrated a 120-Gbps wireless link over 15 cm at −17.2-dB EVM, with 32-dB peak conversion gain, 9.5-dB minimum noise figure, and 230-mW power consumption in a 7.5-mm² die. The work was featured by UC Irvine News, the University of California, and EurekAlert! (AAAS).

Ultra-Wideband Low-Jitter Ring-VCO Phase-Locked Loop

Manuscript under review · Patent pending

A compact ring-VCO charge-pump PLL with a phase-domain dynamic-feedback architecture that suppresses both reference- and VCO-induced phase noise across a multi-octave tuning range. I carried out the full flow: MATLAB loop and phase-noise modeling, transistor-level design, layout and post-layout verification, PCB design, and silicon measurement. Measured results will be posted after publication.

20-Gb/s Half-Rate Clock and Data Recovery

22-nm FD-SOI

A low-power half-rate CDR with a linear phase detector, duty-cycle correction, and a 10-GHz clock path, consuming 15 mW in total. It includes a 7-bit capacitor-bank LC VCO tuning 9.3–10.8 GHz at about 8 mW, and achieved 452-fs integrated RMS random jitter.

Power-Efficient 90–130-GHz Receiver Front End

22-nm FD-SOI · IMS 2024

A wideband front end with a five-stage stagger-tuned LNA, down-conversion mixer, baseband amplifier, and on-chip frequency tripler. The current-reusing complementary (inverter-based) LNA with CPW interstage matching is the first reported inverter-based LNA above 100 GHz, with 14.8-dB gain, 35-GHz bandwidth, and 6.5-dB minimum noise figure at 7.5 mW.

Millimeter-Wave RF-QAM Transmitter Architecture

TCAS-I 2023 (2024 Darlington Best Paper Award), ISCAS 2023

A transmitter that synthesizes high-order QAM directly in the RF domain by vector-summing binary-scaled QPSK signals. This eliminates the high-speed DAC and relaxes power-amplifier linearity requirements, two of the largest power costs in millimeter-wave transmitters.

Transceiver Link Modeling and RF Specification Budgeting

TCAS-I 2022, OJ-CAS 2023, ISCAS 2025

Analytical models of BER, EVM, and out-of-band-emission degradation caused by PLL jitter and reference spurs, link noise, circuit nonlinearity, and bandwidth limits. The models give phase-noise and linearity budgets for PLLs and power amplifiers that meet a target spectral emission mask, and a closed-form optimum PA output power that minimizes BER in QAM links, validated for 16-, 64-, and 256-QAM.
