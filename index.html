Mohammad Oveisi

Ph.D. Candidate, Electrical and Computer Engineering, UC Irvine

RF / Analog / Mixed-Signal Integrated Circuits · mm-Wave / THz Systems · Frequency Synthesis

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of California, Irvine, working with Prof. Payam Heydari in the Nanoscale Communication Integrated Circuits (NCIC) Labs. I expect to graduate in December 2026.

I design RF, analog, and mixed-signal integrated circuits: phase-locked loops, VCOs, clock-and-data-recovery circuits, and millimeter-wave transceiver front ends. I take them from system-level modeling through transistor-level design, layout, tape-out, and silicon measurement. I also work at the boundary between circuits and systems, turning BER, EVM, and spectral-mask targets into phase-noise, jitter, and linearity budgets for PLLs and power amplifiers.

I have spent four summers (2023–2026) at Qualcomm Technologies designing RF and analog circuits in advanced CMOS for commercial wireless products. My work on an RF-domain QAM transmitter architecture received the 2024 IEEE Transactions on Circuits and Systems Darlington Best Paper Award.

I received my M.Sc. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from UC Irvine in 2023 and my B.Sc. in Electrical Engineering from Sharif University of Technology in 2020.
